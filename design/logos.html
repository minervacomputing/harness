The ruled owl

A round owl in front of a navy disc crossed by ledger rules. A thin gap cuts the owl free of the disc and stops the rules. The name is set in Archivo Expanded ExtraBold capitals.

It is always one colour: navy on light surfaces and near-white on dark ones. Switch on Grid to see the construction.
