Principles
  • 01Owl-blue neutrals and Plex type. Texture only in brand moments.
  • 02Square corners and visible edges. Buttons stay flat, with no shadows.
  • 03Status is a dot and a word. No pills, no coloured stripes.
  • 04Dense, ruled tables with sentence-case headers and tabular numbers.
  • 05Text never sits on texture. Every text colour meets WCAG AA on its surface.
Watch out: square corners and strong edges can feel stiff in long forms. Watch inputs and dialogs.
